Digital Audio Equalizer on FPGA

5-band FIR filter bank · 4-level PWM DAC

Published at AADECA 2023

← Back to portfolio
Published · AADECA 2023

Overview

An FPGA implementation of a 5-band parallel graphic audio equalizer using a symmetric systolic FIR filter bank and a 4-level differential PWM-based output DAC topology. The design achieves high audio quality at 44.1 kHz sampling with one DSP48 slice per filter and latency of 1.5 ms per mono channel.

Problem

Digital audio equalization typically requires high-order FIR filters that consume significant FPGA resources, and an output DAC with enough resolution for high-fidelity audio — both within a limited hardware budget. A parallel filter bank also needs to avoid phase error accumulation and quantization noise between bands.

Solution

A 5-band parallel graphic equalizer built around symmetric systolic FIR filters — each band implemented with one Xilinx DSP48 slice thanks to oversampling and pipelining. The output uses a 4-level differential PWM-based DAC: each half-cycle's 12-bit sample is split into 7 LSBs and 5 MSBs driving two PWMs, summed with a weighted differential analog stage and filtered with a 3rd-order Sallen-Key low-pass filter. The input stage uses active precision rectification and parallel sampling of both half-cycles to increase dynamic range and reject common-mode noise.

Architecture

  • Bands: 5-band parallel graphic EQ (150 Hz, 700 Hz, 2 kHz, 4 kHz, 8 kHz)
  • Filters: FIR, order 170 per band, symmetric systolic structure, one DSP48 slice per filter
  • Fixed-point format: I1Q12 (1 integer, 12 fractional), truncation blocks between stages
  • Sampling rate: 44.1 kHz
  • Input stage: analog preamp (+6 dB), active precision rectifier, parallel sampling of in-phase and counter-phase half-cycles
  • Output stage: 4-level differential PWM DAC (7 LSB + 5 MSB per half-cycle), carrier 705.6 kHz from a 90.3168 MHz system clock, 1:256 resistor ratio, 3rd-order Sallen-Key LPF
  • Coefficients: designed in MATLAB Filter Designer, quantized
  • Target: Artix-7 XC7A35T (Digilent Basys 3)

My role

Design and implementation of the FIR filter bank (systolic structure, fixed-point format, truncation stages), the 4-level differential PWM DAC topology, the analog input conditioning (precision rectifier, parallel sampling), RTL synthesis and timing closure in Vivado, hardware validation on the Basys 3 platform, and authoring of the resulting publication.

Results

  • 5-band FIR filter bank, order 170 per band, one DSP48 slice per filter
  • Latency of approximately 1.5 ms per mono filter (well below the ~10 ms audio standard)
  • Noise floor of −60 dBVrms
  • Ripple < ±2 dB across most of the measured spectrum
  • Novel 4-level differential PWM-based output DAC topology with 1:256 resistor weighting
  • Published in Proc. 28th AADECA Congress, 2023

Publication
