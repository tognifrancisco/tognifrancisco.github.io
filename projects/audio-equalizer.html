Digital Audio Equalizer on FPGA

160-tap FIR filter bank · 4-level PWM DAC

Published · AADECA 2023

Overview

An FPGA implementation of a digital audio equalizer using a multi-band FIR filter bank and a 4-level PWM-based output DAC topology. The design achieves high audio quality at 44.1 kHz sampling with low FPGA resource usage.

Problem

Digital audio equalization typically requires high-order FIR filters that consume significant FPGA resources. Additionally, the output stage needs a DAC with enough resolution for high-fidelity audio. Both challenges must be solved with limited hardware budget.

Solution

A 160-tap FIR filter bank architecture optimized for parallel processing in the FPGA, combined with a 4-level PWM-based output DAC topology that achieves the required effective resolution using only digital output pins and a simple analog reconstruction filter.

Architecture

  • Filter: 160-tap FIR filter bank
  • Sampling rate: 44.1 kHz
  • Output stage: 4-level PWM-based DAC topology
  • Target: FPGA (Verilog / HDL-RTL)
  • Design goal: low FPGA resource usage while maintaining audio quality

My role

Design and implementation of the FIR filter bank, PWM DAC topology, RTL synthesis, hardware validation, and authoring of the resulting publication.

Results

  • 160-tap FIR filter bank at 44.1 kHz sampling
  • Low FPGA resource usage
  • Novel 4-level PWM-based output DAC topology
  • Published in Proc. 28th AADECA Congress, 2023
