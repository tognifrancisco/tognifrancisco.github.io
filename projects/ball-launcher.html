Overview

A professional-grade ball launcher designed for trinquete and frontón training. The system automates ball delivery with precise control over exit speed, spin (top/back), tilt, and azimuth, enabling coaches to reproduce specific game scenarios with high repeatability.

Problem

Professional trinquete training requires consistent, repeatable ball delivery at varying speeds, angles, and spins. Manual throwing cannot achieve the precision or endurance needed for high-performance training. The client needed a system that could reproduce any court scenario on demand, with an intuitive operator interface.

Solution

A distributed dual-MCU mechatronic system with dedicated control nodes, an industrial communication bus, and an operator HMI. The mechanical design uses universal DC series motors with encoder feedback and independent actuation for each degree of freedom.

Architecture

  • Engine master: TI TMS320F280049 (C2000) — real-time motor control loops
  • Supervisor engine: STM32 (ARM Cortex-M) — system orchestration, HMI interface, telemetry
  • Communication: Modbus RTU between nodes and HMI
  • Motion axes: exit speed, top spin, back spin, tilt, azimuth
  • Sensing: Allegro current sensors + quadrature encoders coupled to DC series motors
  • HMI: court zone, bounce angle, and spin configuration

My role

Full-system design and development: mechanical concept and CAD, electrical design (PCB, wiring, protections), embedded firmware for both MCUs, Modbus RTU network, HMI integration, sensor telemetry, system integration, and validation with the client.

Results

  • Ball exit speed up to 200 km/h
  • Independent control of top spin, back spin, tilt, and azimuth
  • Repeatable delivery for training scenarios defined via HMI
  • Complete mechanical, electrical, and 3D design ready for manufacturing
