Selected Projects

Low-Latency Fault Detection for Power Inverters

2024 – 2026 · Published at CAE 2026

FPGA-based architecture for open-switch fault detection in power semiconductors used in electric drives. Detection latency of 10% of the electrical period, using approximately 6% of LUTs and 5% of flip-flops on an Artix-7 XC7A35T at 100 MHz. Validated through MATLAB simulation, Proteus, hardware-in-the-loop, and bench testing on a 0.75 kW induction motor drive.

FPGA · Verilog · Xilinx Vivado · DSP · Power Electronics

Amplitude-Dependent Nonlinear System Identification via Volterra Kernels and PCA

2026 · Preprint · Validation phase

Method for identifying and synthesizing amplitude-dependent nonlinear systems. Identification via exponential swept-sine excitation at multiple amplitudes, with up to 5 Volterra kernels extracted per measurement. Continuous amplitude model via PCA with shape-scale separation. Synthesis based on fixed FIR filter banks weighted by interpolated scores. Real-time implementation on DSP/FPGA is future work.

Volterra Kernels · PCA · ESS · MATLAB · DSP

Professional Ball Launcher — Trinquete

End-to-end mechatronic system for professional training. Distributed dual-MCU architecture (TI TMS320F280049 + STM32), Modbus RTU, multi-axis control up to 200 km/h, HMI, sensor telemetry.

Digital Audio Equalizer on FPGA

2023 · Published at AADECA 2023

5-band FIR filter bank (order 170 per band) with symmetric systolic structure and 4-level PWM-based output DAC topology. One DSP48 slice per filter. 44.1 kHz sampling, 1.5 ms latency, noise floor −60 dBVrms, ripple < ±2 dB.

FPGA · Verilog · FIR · DSP · PWM DAC · Audio

Time-Domain Pitch Shifting for Real-Time Audio

2023 – 2025

Design and validation of two time-domain pitch shifting methods for embedded platforms. Doppler-based shifter with two crossfaded read pointers, implemented in C on ARM Cortex-M4F (Tiva C TM4C123G) at 44.1 kHz with 2 ms latency. Weighted granular decimation of an oversampled buffer (L=4^n), validated in MATLAB simulation at 44.1 kHz. Preprint. Hardware implementation on STM32 BlackPill planned.

TI TM4C · ARM Cortex-M · MATLAB · DSP · Real-Time Audio

Multi-Channel Temperature Control for Industrial Lab Flasks

2026

Six-channel simultaneous temperature control with SSR burst-fire strategy (500 ms window), NTC sensing, STM32 firmware, local UI with 7-segment display and rotary encoder.

STM32 · SSR · NTC · Industrial Control · Embedded C
